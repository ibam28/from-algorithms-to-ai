Opening question

"AI", "Machine Learning", "Algorithms" — what's the difference?

These three terms are used interchangeably, but they mean different things. As a result, many people are confused — and some are even scared of AI.

📐

Algorithms

Definite steps to solve a problem.

🤖

Machine Learning

How computers learn from data instead of being given rules.

✨

AI

The widest umbrella: machines that act "intelligently".

We'll explore each one — starting with what's most familiar.

Slide ini adalah "peta". Jangan jelaskan ketiganya sekarang — cukup tunjukkan bahwa ada tiga hal berbeda. Tujuan: audiens tahu ke mana kita akan pergi.

Part 1 · Algorithms

An algorithm is like a recipe

" Definite steps: stir 5 minutes, bake at 180°C, wait 20 minutes. Follow the order, and the result is predictable.

Key point: algorithms are not just for computers. Recipes, routes, counting change — those are algorithms too.

Part 2 · Machine Learning

ML is a chef who learns from 10,000 tries

" This chef doesn't read recipes. They cook, try, fail, improve — thousands of times. Eventually they just know: "if the batter is like this, bake it this long".

A regular algorithm

Given rules → follows them → result.

Machine Learning

Given data + answers → discovers its own rules → result.

This is the core difference: rules vs data.

Ini adalah slide paling penting. Pembeda "aturan vs data" adalah inti dari machine learning. Kalau audiens paham ini, sisanya jadi mudah. Gunakan gerakan tangan: satu tangan menunjuk "aturan ditulis manusia", tangan lain menunjuk "aturan ditemukan mesin".

Part 2 · Machine Learning

You already use ML every day

Unlike regular algorithms, ML learns from patterns instead of fixed rules.

📧

Spam filter

Learns from millions of emails: which are spam, which are not.

🎬

Movie recommendations

Learns from what you watch and from people like you.

💳

Fraud detection

Learns normal transaction patterns, then flags suspicious ones.

🏠

House price prediction

Learns from thousands of sales: location, size, year, price.

Empat contoh ini dipilih karena semua orang pernah mengalaminya. Tekankan kata "belajar" setiap kali. Tujuannya agar audiens merasa ML bukan hal asing — mereka berinteraksi dengannya tiap hari.

Part 3 · AI

AI isn't new — it's 70+ years old

  • 1950 Alan Turing proposes the "Turing Test" — can a machine fool a human?
  • 1956 Dartmouth Conference — the term "Artificial Intelligence" is officially born.
  • 1997 Deep Blue (IBM) beats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
  • 2012 AlexNet wins ImageNet in a landslide — the deep learning era begins.
  • 2017 The Transformer architecture is introduced — the foundation of modern language models.
  • 2022 ChatGPT brings generative AI to millions of regular people in weeks.
Timeline ini menunjukkan AI bukan tren sesaat. Sudah puluhan tahun. Warna titik menandakan era: biru = AI klasik, oranye = ML bangkit, ungu = AI generatif. Kalau ditanya soal tahun-tahun spesifik, jawab dengan tenang: ini milestone besar, bukan daftar lengkap.

Part 4 · Relationship

So how do the three relate?

AI
ML
Algorithms
AI — widest: machines acting "intelligently"
Machine Learning — subset of AI: learns from data
Algorithms — the tool ML uses to work
Symbolic AI — inside AI, outside ML (human-written if-then rules).

Not a straight line — a hierarchy: Algorithms ⊂ ML ⊂ AI.

Diagram konsentris adalah jawaban visual utama. Kunci: tidak semua AI adalah ML (ada symbolic AI). Tidak semua algoritma dipakai untuk AI. Kalau audiens hanya mengingat satu slide, ini yang harus mereka ingat.

Part 4 · Relationship

To avoid misunderstanding

Three counter-examples showing we aren't oversimplifying.

Example Proves
Symbolic AI (expert system, rule-based) AI can exist without ML — rules written by humans, not learned.
Quantum algorithm (Shor's algorithm) Algorithms can exist without AI — pure math tools.
Classic linear regression A statistical algorithm used by ML, but ML is much more than that.

This is why we use a hierarchy, not just a list.
