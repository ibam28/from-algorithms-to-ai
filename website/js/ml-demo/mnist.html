MNIST Digit Recognizer

A real CNN (Convolutional Neural Network) that runs entirely in your browser. No server, no API calls — all the machine learning happens on your device.

⏱️ Estimate: ~30 seconds of training on a modern laptop, ~1-2 minutes on a phone. After training, draw any digit on the canvas and watch the model predict.

01

🏋️ Train Model

The model is trained from the MNIST dataset (subset of 5,000 images) right in your browser.

Model Architecture

  • Conv2D 16 filters (3×3) → MaxPool 2×2
  • Conv2D 32 filters (3×3) → MaxPool 2×2
  • Flatten → Dense 128 → Dense 10
  • Total: ~108K parameters

ℹ️ About This Demo

  • Training happens in the browser — not on a server. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Subset of 5,000 of 60,000 training images is used to keep the demo fast.
  • Typical accuracy: 90-95% on the subset (full MNIST: 98%+ with more epochs).
  • Limitation: first visit downloads TF.js (~1.2 MB), training ~30 seconds.
